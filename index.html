Frequently Asked

How far in advance should I book?

+

We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance for birthdays and 1–3 months for weddings.

Do you provide floral and balloon services?

+

Yes! We offer fresh floral arrangements, artificial flower setups, and organic balloon garlands.

What areas do you serve?

+

We primarily serve Tamil Nadu — Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, and surrounding areas.
